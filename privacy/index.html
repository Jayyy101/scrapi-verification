Scrapi Privacy Policy

Policy version: November 1, 2026.

Who operates Scrapi

Scrapi is operated by Jay Ma. This initial private beta is intended for adults aged 18 and over in the United States. Please do not join this beta if you are under 18. This is the intended audience; the current app does not verify age or enforce a country restriction.

For privacy questions, contact machaojie0611@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, sign-in links or private invite codes by email.

Information you provide

When you create an account, you provide an email address, password and display name. Supabase handles authentication and email verification for Scrapi. The app uses your account identity and session to keep your data associated with you.

You can save place names, addresses, categories, optional visit dates, notes, ratings and recommendations. Selected places may include map coordinates, a structured address and an Apple place identifier. You can also create Scrapbook names and Outing titles, dates, descriptions, stops and attendance labels.

You can choose photos from your library or tap Take photo. Camera permission is requested only when you choose Take photo. You can use or retake the photo; Scrapi does not automatically save captured photos to your device photo library.

For selected or captured photos, Scrapi prepares resized images and thumbnails and removes embedded EXIF and similar metadata from those prepared copies before uploading. The visible image can still reveal people, locations or other private information. Scrapi uses only the photos you select for the feature; it does not upload your entire photo library. Your original Photos-library images are not modified or deleted by Scrapi's preparation or account-deletion process.

Scrapi stores membership and join-request information, roles, authorship, dates of actions and technical identifiers needed to associate content and prevent duplicate saves. It also keeps recovery information so an interrupted operation can be checked without creating the same memory again.

Maps and location

Scrapi uses Apple's map and place-search services. Search text and the context needed for a map/search request are processed through Apple's services. If you have already allowed location access, Scrapi uses your current or a recent valid position to initialize the map. It does not keep recentering after you pan. The Nearby filter uses its own separately chosen center. The native map can show your position and its accuracy indication. Tapping Recenter or requesting Nearby can ask for location permission; opening the map does not itself ask. This beta does not implement continuous background location tracking. You can deny location access and enter places manually.

The location of a place you deliberately save can be stored with that place. It is separate from an ongoing record of your device's movements. Apple handles its service data under Apple's privacy information.

What is private and what is shared

My Places memories, notes and photos are accessible through Scrapi only to your account. Choosing a shared Scrapbook creates a separate destination copy. Shared notes and photos explicitly included in that Scrapbook are available to its current members. New members need the owner's approval after requesting access with an invite code. Pending applicants do not receive the Scrapbook's private content.

Members can see shared contributors' display names, the current membership roster and Outing attendance labels. Owners can see pending applicants' display names and manage membership and shared contributions. Owning a Scrapbook does not give someone access to another member's My Places notes or photos.

Edits and deletion in one destination do not automatically edit an independent copy in another. Leaving or being removed from a Scrapbook ends access but does not itself delete contributions or historical Outing attendance labels. Account deletion has the different effects explained below.

Other members may capture or independently copy information they can view. Scrapi cannot recall screenshots or copies outside its control. “Private” describes the app's access restrictions, not end-to-end encryption: the service operator and infrastructure providers can process data when needed to operate, secure or support the service. Do not use this beta for highly sensitive information.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, store and display your memories, enforce your chosen sharing destinations, manage membership, show maps and Outings, recover interrupted work, investigate bugs and respond to feedback. Technical receipts and identifiers prevent duplicate operations and help distinguish a saved memory from an uncertain response. The app does not copy a private note into shared notes automatically.

Information on your device

Scrapi keeps a sign-in session, preferences and account-specific recovery records on the device. Prepared photo copies can remain while their uploads need attention; the app releases completed copies when all work using them is finished. Recovery may retain identifiers and file references after a save. Some unsaved work is kept only in memory and may be lost when the app closes. The app does not promise to restore every unsaved draft.

Normal sign-out does not erase all recovery records. They are kept for the same account so uncertain work can be checked later. After verified deletion, Finish on this device in the updated app clears that account's recovery records and prepared photo copies and signs it out locally. We cannot remotely wipe an offline device, an old app version, device backups or your original photo library.

Deleting memories or your account

You can start account deletion in Profile → Privacy & sharing → Delete Account. The app shows what will be affected and asks you to confirm your identity and choice. You do not need to send an email to start the normal deletion process.

Account deletion removes your account/profile, private Places and memories, your memories in shared Scrapbooks (including books you have left), your uploaded photos, and Outings you created. Removing an Outing removes its arrangement and links; other people's independent memories remain unless you explicitly close the whole Scrapbook containing them. Your attendance/name is removed from other Outings.

If you own a Scrapbook, you can transfer it to a current member to keep it available. Transfer does not keep a copy of your deleted memories or make the new owner their author. Alternatively, you can explicitly close the Scrapbook. The app warns that closure removes its shared content for everyone, including other people's memories in that book. It does not delete anyone's My Places or copies in other books.

Other people's independent content and shared place/book records needed for it can remain in surviving Scrapbooks, without your profile or creator attribution where it can be detached. Contact us if remaining collaborative text contains personal information that needs review. We cannot erase independent screenshots or copies held by other people.

Jay checks deletion requests daily and completes deletion **within 7 days of the original request** when the confirmed impact remains current and you have provided any confirmation needed to proceed. A pending request does not remove content or stop access. You can cancel in the app until processing starts.

If content, membership or ownership changes affect what would be deleted, the app shows Review needed. Open Review updated impact, inspect the current counts and owned Scrapbooks, explicitly choose any whole-Scrapbook closures or complete ownership transfers, and confirm Confirm updated impact with your password. This updates the same request; you do not need to cancel or start over, and the original seven-day deadline does not restart. Jay cannot confirm changed impact on your behalf or treat silence as consent.

Jay follows up each day an unresolved request needs attention and contacts you through the email address associated with your account if your action is needed. If changes keep interfering, Jay helps arrange a time to review and process the request together. If missing confirmation or a technical problem prevents completion within seven days, Jay explains the reason and next action by that deadline, keeps the request open unless you cancel, and continues daily follow-up. An update or missed deadline is not a claim that deletion is complete. We do not repeatedly cancel and replace your request or silently expand the approved deletion.

When processing starts, normal account access stops and cancellation is no longer possible. Jay verifies removal of the exact database content, photo files and account identity. The app shows pending, review needed, processing and confirmed completion separately. An unconfirmed response preserves the same request receipt without submitting again. Check status in the app, including from the Privacy Policy screen after sign-out. Completion is shown only after removal from active systems is verified; closing the app does not cancel the request.

Retention and remaining copies

Cloud memories remain available until removed through the app or account deletion. Removing a photo from view blocks app access; deletion of its stored image and thumbnail requires a separate cleanup step. Recovery, operational logs, feedback email, local development/acceptance evidence and backups can have different lifetimes. We have retained local application backups.

Jay retains the minimum deletion and ownership record needed to prevent deleted content from returning when a backup is restored. Before a restored database is made available, it must be reconciled against those records, account identity and photo storage. If the latest records or reconciliation cannot be confirmed, the restored app stays unavailable. These records are not a copy of your notes or photos, but technical identifiers can still be personal information.

Recovery receipts and prepared photo copies on a device remain until their work is settled or that device verifies account deletion. The app does not remotely erase an offline device. A deletion status receipt may become unavailable later; an unavailable receipt does not itself prove completion. Contact Jay to reconcile it.

Support email, development and acceptance evidence, provider logs and retained backups are separate from the active account. There is not yet a verified fixed removal schedule for all these records. Provider-controlled retention depends on provider policies and account settings; retained local backups and support records may also outlast active-account deletion. We do not promise that every such record will disappear within seven days. Contact Jay to request review of remaining records and an explanation of what can be removed and any limits.

Your choices and policy changes

You can choose where memories are saved, omit photos/location, change iOS permissions, edit or delete content that you are allowed to manage, leave a Scrapbook, and use the account-deletion flow in the updated app. Contact the email above with privacy questions or requests about data outside those controls.

We will update this policy when the app's practices change and show its version date. Material changes affecting the private beta should be communicated to testers before relying on the changed practice.
